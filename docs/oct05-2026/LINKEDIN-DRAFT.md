## Exact Post Text

An AI visibility score can look precise and still miss the customers you're trying to reach. The questions behind it decide what you're measuring.

Think about a roofing company that does both home repairs and commercial work. If most of the questions are about a leaking house, the report tells you very little about the facilities manager looking for someone to replace a warehouse roof.

Before using that score to decide where to spend money, we'd check four things:

- Do the questions cover the products, services and audiences that matter to the business?
- Are we missing people comparing providers, buying in a particular location or dealing with a different situation?
- Are we comparing the same questions over time, or did the test change?
- Are these possible questions we've modeled, or evidence of what people actually asked?

Searchable's new Prompt Universe brings attention to this selection work. Its question map models what buyers could ask; the number of prompts isn't a count of real searches.

Whatever tool you use, ask to see a few answers behind the score. That gives you something specific to discuss before commissioning more content or another round of website changes.
