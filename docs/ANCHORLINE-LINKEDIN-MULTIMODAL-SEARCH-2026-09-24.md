# Visual Search Is Now Visible in Search Console

Search isn't always a person typing words into a box anymore.

People use a phone camera, upload an image, circle an object on a screen, and ask systems to explain what they see. Google Search Console can now show when some of that image-led behavior reaches pages on your site.

That does not create a new ranking trick. It creates a better question for the team: which pages are being discovered visually, and do those pages actually explain what the visitor needs to understand?

The answer is usually not more decorative imagery. It is stronger visual evidence, clear page context, accessible technical implementation, and a useful next action.

For a product business, that may be a useful comparison or a clear product view. For a local business, it may be a recognizable place or service outcome. For B2B, it may be the diagram that finally makes a complicated process understandable.

Visual clarity is part of search clarity.
